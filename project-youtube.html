Project Overview

Learn about the strategy, tools, and achievements behind this educational content platform.

This project involved creating and managing a YouTube channel focused on producing high-quality educational video essays. Starting from zero, I built a growing community of learners interested in science, history, and global affairs. The channel has achieved 7,056 total views and 284.1 hours of cumulative watch time, demonstrating strong audience retention and engagement.

Through data-driven content strategy and consistent audience engagement, I developed expertise in content planning, video production, audience analysis, and community management. The project demonstrates the ability to create compelling educational narratives that resonate across diverse geographic audiences.
